7.1 The Biology of Cellular Senescence

Cellular senescence is a state of stable cell-cycle arrest that occurs in response to diverse stresses, including telomere attrition, DNA damage, oncogene activation, and mitochondrial dysfunction. In a finished manuscript, this area carries the core academic narrative with numbered citations and carefully controlled paragraph lengths.

CLINICAL APPLICATION

Reducing pathological senescent-cell burden is an emerging therapeutic strategy intended to improve organ function and extend healthspan in aging individuals.
