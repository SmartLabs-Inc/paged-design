The Legacy Thesis

Three levers. Not one.

Everyone sells you the first lever. The money is in the other two.

Lever 01

Keep more of it

Top line is the most expensive way to fix a business. Margin leakage, working-capital drag, entity structure, and deal-stage tax exposure quietly cost more than a bad sales month — and unlike revenue, every dollar you stop losing drops straight into the multiple.

Lever 02

Automate the systems

A buyer isn't purchasing your effort — they can't inherit it. They're buying documented process, instrumented data, and workflows that execute without a hero. Automation isn't a cost-cutting story. It's the proof that the machine is transferable.

Lever 03

Become acquirable

Acquirability is a manufactured condition: clean books, contracted revenue, diversified customers, a second-in-command, assigned IP, transferable contracts. Build those and you stop hoping for an offer — you start choosing between them.

Lever One, In Detail

Where the money actually leaks.

Before you chase another dollar of revenue, close the holes. These are the five leaks we find in almost every workbook — and each one compounds, because it hits earnings and the multiple applied to them.

Leak Pricing drift & discount creep

Rates set three years ago, exceptions granted and never rescinded, a discount schedule nobody owns. A 3-point gross-margin recovery on mid-seven-figure revenue is usually the single largest EBITDA line available — and it costs nothing to capture.

Leak Working capital sitting still

Slow collections, stale inventory, terms you never renegotiated. It doesn't show up in the P&L, so it never gets managed — but it is cash you already earned, and buyers set a working-capital peg at close that will price your bad habits back at you.

Leak Entity structure left on default

The structure that suited a startup rarely suits a sale. Entity type, holding structure, basis, and qualification windows are decisions with calendar deadlines — most of the good options close 24 to 60 months before a transaction, not after LOI.

Leak Unmodeled deal-stage tax

Asset sale versus stock sale, allocation, state apportionment, installment treatment, rollover equity. The difference between a modeled and an unmodeled exit is frequently seven figures on the same headline price.

Fix Manual work that should be machine work

Every recurring task done by a human hand is a payroll line, an error rate, and a key-person dependency in one. Automating it removes cost, removes risk, and produces the exact artefact a buyer wants: a process that runs whether or not anyone shows up.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Is this a real valuation?
No — and anyone telling you a web form produces a certified valuation is selling something. This is a directional model built on observed market multiple ranges, scale adjustments, and readiness weightings. Its job is to show you the shape of the opportunity and where your value is trapped. A formal valuation comes later, and it comes from a credentialed appraiser.
Why does the exit date change my number so much?
Because value creation needs runway. Recurring revenue has to be contracted and then demonstrated. A second-in-command has to be hired and then proven. Clean financials need trailing periods, not a good quarter. Tax structure has qualification windows measured in years. Under twelve months, most of what's available is defensive — protecting the number you already have. Past twenty-four months, you can genuinely manufacture a different business.
My revenue is strong. Why is my multiple low?
Buyers don't buy revenue, they buy transferable earnings with acceptable risk. Revenue that depends on the owner's relationships, concentrates in two clients, or has to be re-won every month is discounted hard — sometimes to the point where a smaller, boring, contracted business trades at double your multiple.
Isn't "keeping more" just cost cutting?
Cost cutting is one small part of it, and often the least valuable. The bigger money is in pricing integrity, working-capital discipline, structure, and deal-stage tax planning. Those don't shrink the business — they stop it from bleeding, and every recovered dollar of earnings is then multiplied at exit.
What if I'm not sure I want to sell?
Then build it as though you will. Every driver in this workbook — less owner dependence, recurring revenue, real management, automated systems, clean books — makes the business better to own. Acquirability is just operational excellence with a buyer's name on the report card. You keep the optionality either way.
Does anything I type here get sent to you?
No. The entire model runs locally in your browser. There is no analytics call, no form submission, and no network request of any kind in this page. If you want us to see your numbers, you send them deliberately — using the buttons on the results panel.
